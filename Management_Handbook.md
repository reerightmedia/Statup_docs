## 2. ONBOARDING, CREDENTIAL DELEGATION & DPDP COMPLIANCE

To initiate social media management services safely, Reeright Media strictly enforces secure platform delegation protocols.

### 2.1 Access Delegation Checklist:
* **Meta Business Suite Access:** Client shall grant Partner Access to Reeright Media’s Business Manager ID for Facebook Page and Instagram Business Account.
* **Direct Credentials Security:** Where direct login is required (e.g., standalone Instagram accounts), credentials must be shared via encrypted password links. Credentials are stored in enterprise password vaults enforcing Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
* **Asset Repository:** Client shall provide a dedicated Google Drive folder containing vector logos, brand identity guidelines, raw photo/video assets, and product specs.

### 2.2 DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Data Protection:
* **Data Fiduciary vs. Processor:** Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Client acts as the **Data Fiduciary** and Reeright Media acts as the **Data Processor**.
* **Lead Form Data:** When processing customer lead forms, DM inquiries, or giveaway entries on behalf of Client, Client warrants that explicit user consent has been captured in compliance with the DPDP Act.
* **Data Minimization & Purging:** Reeright Media processes customer lead data strictly for campaign execution and purges temporary local lead spreadsheets within 30 days of campaign completion.

## 3. MONTHLY CONTENT PRODUCTION LIFECYCLE & APPROVAL SLA

To maintain consistent posting without compromising quality, Reeright Media adheres to a strict 30-day production workflow.

```
┌───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Phase                     │ Timeline & Milestone                                   │
├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Strategy & Briefing    │ 20th–25th of Previous Month                            │
│ 2. Creative & Reel Edit   │ 26th–30th of Previous Month                            │
│ 3. Client Review Window   │ 1st–3rd of Current Month (48-Hour SLA)                │
│ 4. Revisions & Tweaks     │ 4th–5th of Current Month                               │
│ 5. Automated Scheduling   │ 6th onward (Publishing Phase)                          │
└───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Content Review & Approval SLA (48-Hour Window):
* Monthly content grids are submitted to Client via a dedicated review portal (Google Drive / Notion) 5 days prior to the start of the publishing month.
* **Review SLA:** Client is granted a **48-hour window** to review and provide written feedback or minor text edits.
* **Deemed Approval Protocol:** If written feedback is not received within **72 hours** of submission, scheduled content for the immediate upcoming days shall be deemed legally approved for publication to protect the brand's algorithmic consistency and posting schedule.

## 4. CONTENT REVISION POLICY & FAIR USAGE

To balance creative excellence with commercial sustainability, revisions are governed strictly under the following categories:

### 4.1 Minor Adjustments (Included in Retainer)
Executed within **24–48 hours** at no additional cost:
* Correcting minor typos, grammar, or caption formatting.
* Adjusting hashtag stacks or Call-To-Action (CTA) links.
* Minor visual adjustments (e.g., tweaking logo placement, swapping an approved photo, or adjusting text alignment).

### 4.2 Major Concept Changes (Scope Creep Protection)
Major concept changes involve altering approved strategies, rescripting edited Reels after initial direction sign-off, or requesting complete visual redesigns of finished posts.
* Major concept changes require additional planning and will be quoted separately per item as set forth in the [Additional Services Guide](file:///f:/Reeright/Additional_Services_Guide.md).

### 4.3 Fair Usage & Monthly Expiration
Monthly retainer deliverables (posts, Reels, Stories) must be consumed within the active billing month. Unused deliverables do not roll over to subsequent months if client-side delays prevent asset collection or brief sign-off.

## 5. ASCI GUIDELINES & BRAND SAFETY COMPLIANCE

Reeright Media maintains strict compliance with the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) codes:

### 5.1 Claim Substantiation:
* Client is solely responsible for ensuring that all product claims (e.g., "100% Organic", "India's #1", "Guaranteed 24-Hour Results", medical or financial claims) provided to Reeright Media are fully backed by scientific, statutory, or factual proof.
* Reeright Media reserves the right to decline publishing any post containing unverified or misleading claims that violate ASCI codes or Indian consumer protection laws.

### 5.2 Influencer Disclosures (#ad, #collab):
* All influencer marketing campaigns managed by Reeright Media will strictly comply with ASCI Guidelines for Influencer Advertising on Digital Media.
* Influencer posts must feature prominent, un-hidden disclosures such as `#ad`, `#sponsored`, or `#collab` across Instagram Reels, Stories, and Feed posts.

## 6. COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT & LEAD MODERATION SOP

For packages including community management (Growth and Elite Plans):

* **Post Comment Replies:** Moderating and replying to general comments on feed posts (Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST).
* **Spam & Moderation:** Removing abusive remarks, competitor spam, and phishing links.
* **Lead Qualification & Escalation:** Greeting leads in Instagram DMs, answering general FAQs (store location, business hours), and forwarding warm sales leads to Client’s designated sales contact via WhatsApp within **2–4 business hours**.
* **Exclusion:** Reeright Media does not handle complex customer support disputes, technical product complaints, or legal notices via social DMs. These will be escalated directly to Client.
