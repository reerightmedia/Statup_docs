## PART 1: 5-MINUTE INSTAGRAM AUDIT FRAMEWORK

Before messaging any prospective client brand, spend 3 minutes reviewing their Instagram page to identify 2–3 clear weaknesses. This makes your pitch personalized, authoritative, and high-converting.

### Quick Audit Checklist:
1. **Bio & Lead Funnel:** Is the bio generic? Does it lack a clear value proposition or clickable link tree?
2. **Reel Quality & Hooks:** Are they posting Reels regularly? Are the Reels missing dynamic subtitles, kinetic typography, or hook text?
3. **Visual Grid Aesthetic:** Does the grid look cluttered, inconsistent in color/font, or unpolished?
4. **ASCI Compliance & Disclosures:** Are influencer posts missing `#ad` / `#collab` disclosures?
5. **Engagement & Response Rate:** Are comments left unanswered? Is there an active Instagram Story right now?
