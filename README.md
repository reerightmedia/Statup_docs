## ⚖️ Legal & Statutory Compliance Highlights

All contracts and operational policies in this repository are engineered specifically for independent advertising and media agencies operating in India:

* **Pre-Paid Retainer Protections (Indian Contract Act, 1872):** All monthly retainers are pre-paid in advance with Net 7 day terms and 18% p.a. late payment interest under the MSMED Act 2006.
* **Conditional IP Assignment (Copyright Act, 1957 Section 19):** Copyright ownership of final exported graphics/videos transfers to the client **only upon 100% receipt of payment**. Source files (.PSD, .PRPROJ, Figma) remain Agency Pre-Existing IP.
* **Data Privacy Protection (DPDP Act, 2023):** Client is designated as the **Data Fiduciary** and Agency as the **Data Processor** for customer leads and DM inquiries.
* **ASCI Advertising Disclosures:** Full compliance guidelines for product claim substantiation and mandatory influencer disclosures (`#ad`, `#collab`).
