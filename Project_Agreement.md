## 1. DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following capitalised terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:

* **1.1 "Ad Spend"** means the direct payments made by Client to Third-Party Platforms (including Meta, Google, LinkedIn) for paid media placements, separate and distinct from Agency’s retainer or management fees.
* **1.2 "Agency Pre-Existing IP"** means all intellectual property, proprietary tools, workflows, design templates, software code, unselected pitch concepts, strategy frameworks, and preliminary sketches created or owned by Agency prior to or independently of this Agreement.
* **1.3 "ASCI Guidelines"** means the codes, guidelines, and disclosure rules issued by The Advertising Standards Council of India, including guidelines for Influencer Advertising on Digital Media and misleading claims substantiation.
* **1.4 "Client Materials"** means all trademarks, trade names, logos, brand guidelines, product samples, images, videos, audio clips, text, customer data, and technical specifications provided by Client to Agency for incorporation into Deliverables.
* **1.5 "Copyright Act"** means the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (as amended from time to time).
* **1.6 "Data Fiduciary" and "Data Processor"** shall have the meanings assigned to them under Section 2 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Client is the Data Fiduciary and Agency acts solely as the Data Processor.
* **1.7 "Deliverables"** means the final, approved social media posts, carousel graphics, edited Reel videos, written captions, and strategic documents created by Agency specifically for Client and delivered under an active Scope of Work. Deliverables explicitly exclude source files, raw unedited footage, and Agency Pre-Existing IP.
* **1.8 "DPDP Act"** means the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, along with any rules notified thereunder.
* **1.9 "Indian Contract Act"** means the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (as amended).
* **1.10 "Monthly Retainer"** means the fixed recurring monthly fee payable in advance by Client to Agency for the ongoing performance of social media management services as specified in the selected package ([Pricing Guide](file:///f:/Reeright/Reeright_Media_Pricing_Guide.md)) or Statement of Work.
* **1.11 "Third-Party Platforms"** means social media networks, advertising portals, or third-party digital infrastructure including Instagram, Facebook, Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp, Google, YouTube, and Shopify.

## 3. COMMERCIAL TERMS, BILLING & FINANCIAL PROTECTION

* **3.1 Pre-Paid Monthly Retainer:** All monthly retainer fees are strictly **payable in advance**. Invoices for upcoming monthly service cycles are issued 5 days prior to the start of the billing period and must be settled in full on or before the due date.
* **3.2 Payment Terms (Net 7 Days):** All invoices issued by Agency are due within **seven (7) calendar days** from the invoice date.
* **3.3 Taxes (GST):** All fees quoted by Agency are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST) and applicable statutory levies. Client shall be responsible for paying GST at the prevailing statutory rate upon presentation of a valid GST invoice.
* **3.4 Late Payment Interest:** Any unpaid invoice balance past the due date shall automatically attract statutory interest under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and/or the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006 at the rate of **1.5% per month (18% per annum)** or the maximum permissible by law, calculated daily from the due date until full payment is received.
* **3.5 Right to Suspend Services for Non-Payment:** If Client fails to pay any invoice within seven (7) calendar days of the due date, Agency reserves the absolute right, without prejudice to any other legal remedy, to:
  * (a) Immediately suspend all content creation, post scheduling, and community management;
  * (b) Pause active Meta/Google paid ad campaigns; and
  * (c) Revoke Client’s access to pending unreleased creative assets.  
  * *Agency shall bear no liability whatsoever for any drop in social media engagement, algorithmic organic reach penalties, or lost ad sales resulting from such service suspension.*
* **3.6 Ad Spend Liability Disclaimer:** Client shall attach its own credit card/payment method directly to Third-Party Platform ad accounts (Meta Ads Manager / Google Ads). Agency’s management fee covers strategy and setup only. Under no circumstances shall Agency be liable for funding, advancing, or reimbursing Client’s Ad Spend.

## 6. DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY (DPDP ACT, 2023)

* **6.1 Data Fiduciary vs. Data Processor Roles:** The parties acknowledge that under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):
  * (a) Client acts as the **Data Fiduciary** determining the purpose and means of processing personal data (e.g., customer leads, DM inquiries, giveaway entries);
  * (b) Agency acts strictly as a **Data Processor** processing personal data solely on behalf of, and as instructed by, Client.
* **6.2 Consent & Lawful Basis Warranty:** Client warrants that all personal data provided to Agency, or collected through lead forms managed by Agency, has been obtained with valid, verifiable, and lawful consent from data principal users in full compliance with the DPDP Act.
* **6.3 Security Measures:** Agency agrees to implement reasonable organizational and technical measures to safeguard personal data processed on behalf of Client against unauthorized access or data breaches.

## 10. TERM, RENEWAL & TERMINATION

* **10.1 Initial Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for an initial commitment period of **three (3) months** ("**Initial Term**") to allow for strategic content optimization and algorithmic compounding.
* **10.2 Automatic Month-to-Month Renewal:** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a **month-to-month basis** under the same terms unless terminated in accordance with Section 10.3.
* **10.3 Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause after the Initial Term by providing at least **thirty (30) calendar days' written notice** via email to the other party prior to the next billing cycle.
* **10.4 Termination for Material Breach:** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach (including non-payment under Section 3) and fails to cure such breach within seven (7) days of receiving written notice of default.

## 11. NON-SOLICITATION OF AGENCY TALENT

* **11.1 Non-Poaching Restriction:** During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following its termination, Client agrees not to directly or indirectly solicit, hire, retain, or engage any employee, graphic designer, video editor, or contractor of Agency who performed services under this Agreement without Agency's prior written consent.
* **11.2 Liquidated Damages (Section 74, Indian Contract Act):** If Client breaches Section 11.1, Client agrees to pay Agency as genuine pre-estimated liquidated damages an amount equal to **50% of the hired individual’s annual compensation** or **₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs)**, whichever is higher, payable immediately.
